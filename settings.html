🎛 Hotswap Overlay Controls

Three surfaces, one set of actions. Top Shortcuts ride the toolbar; the Quick Action Runway sits over the content; Deep Cuts ("···") is the deeper toolbox. The same action may appear on more than one.

Top Toolbar

The rail that slides in at the top of a panel. Position, the L2/L1 selector when a Layer 2 exists, Undo, Redo and ··· are fixed controls — they are always present and cannot be removed or reordered. Everything below configures the region between them.

Toolbar Shortcuts

The configurable middle of the toolbar, available whenever the toolbar is revealed. Narrow panels show fewer; widening restores them. Your saved count and order never change to fit.

Drag across the cutoff. The first 3 visible actions appear on the toolbar; grey rows automatically live in Deep Cuts. Switches control intentional visibility.

··· Deep Cuts

The fixed fallback gateway contains the visible remainder of the ordered list above, plus temporary responsive overflow. When empty it remains enabled and says all actions are on the toolbar.

Quick Action Shortcut Runway

A vertical runway down the right edge of every panel, starting below the top-right corner so the website keeps its own controls.
